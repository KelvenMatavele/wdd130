History

Born from a shared passion for the untamed wild, White Water Rafting began in 1990 with nothing more than two weathered rafts and a fearless crew of guides.

What started as weekend excursions quickly evolved into a calling. Over the decades, we have navigated the most challenging rapids and forged an unbreakable bond with the river.

Today, we stand as pioneers of the whitewater industry, driven by a relentless pursuit of adrenaline and a deep respect for nature. Your greatest adventure begins here.
