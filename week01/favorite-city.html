My Favorite City

My favorite place in the word is the Bazaruto Archipelago in inhambane(mozambique).
It's a stunning paradise with crystal-clear turquoise waters and white sand dunes.

What makes Bazaruto so special is its incredible marine life. it is one of the few places where you can see the rare Dugong(sea cow). I love this place because it is perfect for snorkelling, relaxing, and enjoying nature.
